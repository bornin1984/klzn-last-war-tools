Follow the calculation.

One expected discovery cycle for hunting strategies; one other player’s boss for rally only. Rewards count once per event, and only the discoverer receives the discovery reward and refund.

Own: 10 × (N × hunt reward + own boss reward + discovery reward) / (N × hunt cost + own rally cost − refund)

Others: 10 × (N × hunt reward + discovery reward) / (N × hunt cost − refund)

Rally only: 10 × other boss reward / other boss rally cost

N/A means net stamina is zero or negative, or an extreme input exceeds numeric precision. Such a model cannot describe a finite reward per stamina and is excluded from winner rankings and chart lines.

What these numbers can tell you.

For stamina efficiency, choose the strategy with the highest return for the resource you need. Letting others rally avoids your boss-rally cost but gives up its combat rewards. That trade can favor medals while hunting still wins for drone data.

Rallying your own boss improves your hunting efficiency only when that extra rally’s reward per stamina exceeds the hunt-and-discovery cycle’s efficiency. Leading rallies on others’ bosses wins when its return exceeds both hunting strategies. With equal own/other boss costs and rewards, own-rally efficiency sits between the other two strategies and cannot be the sole winner. The live comparisons above account for your edits.

These are long-run averages. Boss availability, time spent finding targets, daily limits, expired bosses and successful defeats are outside the model. It assumes every discovered boss is defeated and its discovery reward is collected. “Others rally” means you do not join or spend stamina on that boss yourself.
